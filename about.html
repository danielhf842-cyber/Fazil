Our story

Founded by people who came from the industry.

Fazil was started by consultants who had worked on site and in contractors' offices and were tired of generalist recruiters who didn't understand the sector. We built the agency we wished we'd had: specialist, honest and in it for the long term.

Today we operate from London and New York, with dedicated teams across civils, M&E, envelopes, fit-out, refurbishment and project controls.

Our values

Three principles that shape every decision we make.

Honesty

We tell clients when we can't find the person, and candidates when a role isn't right for them. That's the foundation of every relationship we build.

Integrity

We do it the right way, not the fastest way. No CV blasting, no over-promising, no placing someone we wouldn't hire ourselves.

Transparency

Everyone knows where they stand at every stage. No hidden fees, no hidden agendas.

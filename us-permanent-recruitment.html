Evidence-based recruitment, not CV roulette.

Permanent hires for construction contractors and consultancies, from site management to board level — assessed on what actually predicts performance.

Submit a vacancyOur process

A shortlist of three you can interview, not thirty you have to sift.

We interview every candidate ourselves, reference them and check their technical knowledge against the projects they claim. By the time you see a profile, the questions you would normally ask in a first interview have already been answered.

  • Structured competency interview with every candidate
  • Technical check by a consultant who has worked in the sector
  • References taken before the shortlist, not after the offer
  • Salary expectations and notice period confirmed up front
Talk about recruitment

How a permanent search runs.

01

Understand the brief

Not just the job description — the projects, the team, the reporting line and the culture the person is walking into.

02

Map the market

Most of the best people are not on job boards. We approach the passive candidates directly, confidentially, and tell you who said no and why.

03

Assess and shortlist

Three to five interviewed, referenced and technically checked candidates, usually within two to three weeks.

04

Offer and onboarding

We manage the offer, the counter-offer and the notice period, and keep in touch with both sides through the first year.

Home / For Employers / Our approach

A process designed for precision, not speed.

Speed is easy. Anyone can send you twenty CVs by lunchtime. We would rather send you three people who can do the job, and tell you honestly what the market looks like.

Talk to a consultant →Why Fazil

Four steps. Every role, every time.

01

Understand your brief

Not just the job spec — the site, the team, the schedule pressure and the culture the person is walking into. If we think the brief will not work in the market, we say so at this stage.

02

Map the market

We go to the passive candidates who are not on job boards. That is usually where your best hire is. You get a view of who is out there, who is interested and what they are earning.

03

Assess rigorously

Every candidate is interviewed, referenced and technically checked before you see them. A consultant who has worked in the sector asks the questions a generalist would not know to ask.

04

Place and follow up

We manage the offer, the counter-offer and the onboarding, and check in throughout the first year — with you and with the person we placed.

Recruiters who have worked in construction.

Every Fazil consultant has worked on the contractor or consultancy side before moving into recruitment. That is why our shortlists are short: we can tell the difference between a superintendent who has run a $40m package and one who has stood next to someone who did.

  • Sector-specialist consultants, not generalist account managers
  • One named contact from brief to placement
  • Honest feedback on the role, the salary and the market
  • We tell you when we cannot help — and who might
Talk to a consultant →

What we will not do.

Most of what makes a recruiter frustrating to work with is what they do to hit a number. We built Fazil to avoid it.

Send CVs you did not ask for

You will only ever receive people who match the brief we agreed, in the number we agreed.

Approach your staff

We do not recruit from clients. Your people are off limits while we work together and for a year after.

Oversell a candidate

Every profile comes with the gaps as well as the strengths. A placement that lasts is worth more to us than a fee.

Disappear after the start date

The first year is where placements fail. We stay on the account and check in with both sides.

Hide the fee

Terms are agreed in writing before we start. The invoice will not contain anything you have not seen.

Waste a candidate’s time

People who interview with you get feedback either way. It is how we keep the best candidates coming back.

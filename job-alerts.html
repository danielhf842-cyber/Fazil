Be part of shaping your future

Whether you're an experienced specialist or at the start of your career, there has never been a more interesting time to build in construction. Contractors and consultancies are looking for people to lead sites, manage complex programmes and deliver the infrastructure, homes and workplaces the next decade needs.

Here we collect live roles across every construction discipline, in the UK and the US. We bring ambitious people together with the companies and projects that are looking for exactly their skills.

When you're ready to take the next step and make a real difference to what gets built, a Fazil job alert is the simplest way to discover the right opportunity — before everyone else does.

Definition of the right job

A job is right for you if:

  • It's with a contractor or consultancy whose projects match the kind of work you want to be known for — the sector, the scale and the standard.
  • The level, salary and location fit where you are now, and the role has a credible path to where you want to be next.
  • The people you'd be working for are ones we'd be happy to place you with. We don't send you roles we wouldn't take ourselves.

The right society starts with the right people

We're always looking for the right people

Since Fazil was founded, our aim has been to build a culture that creates energy, rewards curiosity and puts care for people at the centre. While we work for commercial success, we want a positive impact on the people we place, the projects they deliver and the communities those projects serve. By strengthening both individuals and companies we help them reach their full potential — and build something better.

Your skills are in demand

The UK needs an estimated 250,000 additional construction workers by 2028, and the US is short more than 400,000 skilled trades and site professionals. Demand for planners, M&E engineers, site managers and quantity surveyors is growing faster than the supply of qualified people.

There is a shortage of qualified candidates across every sector, including the ones with the biggest projects. That makes your experience particularly sought after — and it's why the right roles are often filled before they're ever advertised.

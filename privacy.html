Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Fazil Recruitment Group collects when you use this website or our recruitment services, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. It covers both our United Kingdom and United States operations. Please read it together with our Terms of use and Cookie policy.

1. Who we are

Fazil Recruitment Group Ltd ("Fazil", "we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales, is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, for both our UK and US operations.

  • United Kingdom: Fazil Recruitment Group Ltd, company number 15241926, registered office 168-169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London W1W 5PF. ICO registration [ICO number].
  • United States office: Fazil Recruitment Group Ltd, 57 W 57th St, New York, NY 10019. Our US operations are run by the UK company; there is no separate US entity.
  • Privacy contact: uk@fazil-construction.com

In the UK we are regulated as an employment agency and employment business under the Employment Agencies Act 1973 and the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to run a recruitment service.

WhenWhat

You apply for a roleThe role you applied for, the date, and any notes our consultants add during the process (for example interview feedback or availability).
You create a job alertEmail address, the roles and cities you chose, and how often you want to hear from us.

If you have an account you can see, replace or delete your stored CVs from your account page at any time.

You browse the siteOur hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time) for security and to keep the site running. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers. See the Cookie policy.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, special category data (health, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, trade-union membership, criminal records) unless a specific role lawfully requires it, in which case we will tell you why and ask for your explicit consent.

4. Candidate privacy notice

This section is for people looking for work through Fazil.

  • We never send your details to an employer without telling you first. Before we put you forward for a specific role we will confirm with you that you want to be represented for it.
  • What we share with an employer is limited to what they need to assess you for that role: typically your CV / resume, relevant qualifications and certifications, availability, salary expectations and our consultant's summary. We do not share your contact details until you and the employer agree to proceed.
  • If you are placed on a contract or temporary assignment through our UK employment business, we also process payroll, timesheet and right-to-work data as your employer for that assignment.
  • You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, to correct it, or to stop looking for work for you (see Your rights).
  • References: if you give us referee details, you confirm the referee has agreed to be contacted.

5. Employer and client contacts

If you contact us on behalf of a company we keep your name, job title, work contact details and a record of our dealings with you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing and marketing recruitment services to businesses. You can opt out of marketing at any time by emailing uk@fazil-construction.com.

6. Who we share data with

  • Employers / clients — only as described in section 4, and only for roles you have agreed to be considered for.
  • Service providers who process data on our instructions under a written contract:
    • Supabase — database and user authentication (accounts, applications, job alerts).
    • Netlify — website hosting and contact-form handling.
    • Google Fonts — the fonts on this site are loaded from Google's servers, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.
    • Email, payroll (UK employment business) and IT providers we use to run the business.
  • Regulators, courts and law enforcement where the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor if the business is sold or restructured, under the same protections.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers.

7. International transfers

Because we operate in both the UK and the US, candidate and client data may be accessed by our teams in either country, and our service providers may store data in the United States or the European Economic Area. Where UK data is transferred outside the UK we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or on an adequacy decision where one exists. A copy of the relevant safeguards is available on request.

9. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (a "subject access request").
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, where we have no overriding legal reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to our processing, including objecting to direct marketing at any time.
  • Port the data you gave us to another provider in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis (for example job alerts), without affecting what was done before.

To exercise any of these, email uk@fazil-construction.com from the address on your account, or write to the London address above. We respond within one month. You can also delete your own job alerts from your account page.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

10. Information for United States residents

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon or another state with a consumer-privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information for anything other than providing the service you asked for. To exercise your rights, email uk@fazil-construction.com or call +44 (0)20 7096 5077. We will verify your identity using the email on your account. You may authorise an agent to act for you. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (name, email, phone), professional and employment information (CV / resume, work history, certifications), and internet activity (server logs). Sources: you, and the employers and referees you direct us to. Purposes: as set out in section 3.

11. Security

Data is stored with providers that encrypt it in transit and at rest. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Access to candidate and client data inside Fazil is limited to staff who need it for their role and is protected by row-level security in our database. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever become aware of a breach that is likely to affect you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

This site is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top tells you when it last changed; material changes will be flagged on the site or by email to account holders.

Questions: uk@fazil-construction.com · +44 (0)20 7096 5077.

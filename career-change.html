How it works

Employer-funded. Job-guaranteed.

Our client contractors sponsor each cohort because they need the people. That means no fees for you, a guaranteed interview at the end, and a Fazil consultant supporting you through your first year.

  • Apply online — no construction experience required
  • Aptitude and motivation assessment (not a CV screen)
  • 12-week intensive, mix of classroom and site
  • Placed with a sponsoring contractor on completion

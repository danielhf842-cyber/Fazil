International applicants

Fazil has offices in the United States and the United Kingdom. If you're a construction professional looking to work across the Atlantic, we're one of the few recruiters who can help on both sides.

Talk to us →See live jobs

United Kingdom — London

Our head office and founding division. Roles across England, Scotland and Wales in civil, MEP, envelopes, fit-out, renovation and project controls.

United States — New York & Dallas

Our US division, working with contractors and developers on the East Coast and in Texas. Same specialist model, same consultants' approach.

How it works

We help you make the move — not just find the job.

01

Tell us where you're going

US to UK, or UK to US. We look at your experience, qualifications and the kind of projects you want, and tell you honestly what transfers and what doesn't.

02

We match you with the right employers

Not every contractor sponsors or relocates. We know which ones do, and we only put you forward for roles where the visa and relocation side is realistic.

03

We guide the paperwork

Qualification equivalence (SMSTS vs OSHA 30, CSCS, professional memberships), work authorization (I-9 / E-Verify), references and timing. Our consultants have done it before and walk you through it.

04

We stay with you after you land

Your Fazil consultant checks in throughout your first year in the new country — and our local office is there if anything needs sorting.

One conversation, two markets.

Because we run both offices ourselves, you're not being handed between agencies. The recruiter who understands your background in New York is the same one talking to the hiring manager in London.

If you're not sure whether a move is realistic yet, that's fine — a quick call with a specialist will tell you where you stand.

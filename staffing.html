Skilled people on site when your project needs them.

Contract and temporary construction professionals, from a single site supervisor to a full project team — vetted, compliant and ready to start.

Request contract staffHow it works

Scale your team with the programme, not against it.

Workload in construction is never flat. Staffing lets you bring in the right people for the phase you are in — groundworks, structure, envelope, fit-out, handover — and release them when the phase is done, without carrying the overhead in between.

  • Site managers, supervisors and engineers
  • M&E supervisors and commissioning engineers
  • Quantity surveyors, planners and document controllers
  • Trades and operatives through our vetted supply chain
Talk about staffing

From brief to boots on site in days.

01

Brief us

A ten-minute call covers the role, the site, the start date and the tickets you need. We will tell you straight away whether we can fill it.

02

Shortlist within 48 hours

You see two or three people who are available, local and already interviewed by us — not a pile of CVs.

03

Start on site

We handle contracts, right-to-work, CSCS and insurance checks before day one. Timesheets and payroll run through us.

04

Ongoing support

A named consultant checks in during the first week and stays on the account for the duration of the assignment.

Everything that comes with a contractor, handled.

When you take someone on through Fazil you take on a worker, not an administration project. We carry the compliance so that your site team can get on with the job.

  • Right-to-work, CSCS/CPCS and qualification checks
  • PAYE or compliant umbrella payroll, with IR35 assessed up front
  • Weekly timesheets approved online by your site manager
  • Employer’s and public liability insurance in place
  • Replacement within 48 hours if someone does not work out

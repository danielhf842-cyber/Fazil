Questions and answers

Fazil is a specialist construction staffing and recruitment company operating across the UK and US — from site operatives to board-level appointments, across every corner of the construction & built environment.
We've spent three years earning the trust of the construction industry's leading firms and brightest people, and we're only getting started.
A staffing company employs professionals and places them with clients on a contract or temporary basis, giving employers flexible access to skills as project demands change.
Staffing is flexible, contract-based resourcing built around a programme. Recruitment is a permanent hire, where the candidate joins your business directly after a structured, evidence-based process.
Use the contact page, call our head office, or submit a vacancy or candidate registration directly through the site — a sector specialist will get back to you.
